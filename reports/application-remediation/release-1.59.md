# Application release 1.59

Application release 1.59 advances the generated study data to `private-sector-study-1.55.0` and publishes the independently audited Stream Houston I / The Woodlands, TierPoint Charlotte CL4, and TierPoint / Windstream Little Rock accounts.

The batch adds 51 reported source records and 50 last-resort modeled syntheses after project-specific direct-evidence searches and candidate-by-candidate model review. The study now contains 1,046 source records—940 reported and 106 projected—and 116 separately governed modeled syntheses. Twenty-four completed research audits are map-eligible; analytical completeness remains a separate gate and no project currently passes it.

Charlotte was accepted only after a deeper remediation that added parcel, payment, facility, permit, and power evidence plus three bounded operational and electricity sensitivities. Little Rock required two remediation passes before 44 fiscal arithmetic rows, one minimum-coverage staffing equivalent, and two engineering sensitivities were accepted as explicitly modeled values. Stream retains only direct property and payment evidence because no defensible residual synthesis survived review.
