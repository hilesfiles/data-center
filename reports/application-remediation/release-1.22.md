# Study release 1.22 - Digital Crossroad Hammond financing, jobs and water context

Release `private-sector-study-1.22.0` publishes 455 source-checked economic records across all 36 candidates: 420 reported records and 35 source forecasts. The twenty-second batch adds eight reported records and one forecast to Digital Crossroad DX-1 in Hammond.

## Evidence added

- A contemporaneous November 2020 completion report states that the operating facility employed 17 people. The record remains a dated facility snapshot rather than annual employment, payroll or local-residency evidence.
- Principal's audited fund statements confirm the actual $8.04 million City development loan, mirrored TIF bond, 4% rate and 2044 maturity. The application presents it as financing rather than a grant or general-obligation debt.
- The audited statements report $160,800, $225,800, $258,200 and $264,100 of project TIF revenue used toward repayment in calendar years 2022 through 2025. These pledged/captured tax flows are not added to the parcel's gross taxing-authority distributions or presented as general-fund revenue.
- Hammond's executed 2018 development agreement anticipated approximately 40 full-time jobs. The application retains the figure as a forecast and treats the agreement's local-labor preferences as requirements without realized participation data.
- The agreement states 910.85 MGD of withdrawal capacity and 18.202 MGD of consumptive capacity under IDNR Registration No. 45-01176-EP. Both are labeled permit capacities rather than measured water use.

## Application behavior

The Hammond profile now has 31 records: 28 reported records and three forecasts. The reported view adds a four-year TIF repayment series, loan principal, employee snapshot and two permit-capacity cards while preserving the parcel tax account, separate personal-property assessment, qualified cumulative investment, power capacity and permit-listed backup equipment. The approximately 40-job agreement figure appears only under Plans & forecasts.

## Validation

- Data contract validation covers all schemas and fixtures.
- Private-sector study tests cover Hammond record counts and the employee, financing, annual TIF, water-capacity and forecast distinctions.
- TypeScript and the Vite production build include the million-gallons-per-day unit in the public type contract and display formatter.
- Browser checks cover the expanded Hammond account and narrow-screen evidence view with no runtime errors.

## Remaining limits

The case still lacks annual construction expenditure, construction job-years and payroll, employee wages and retention, local supplier purchases, reconciled recipient-level net fiscal receipts, total debt service and outstanding principal, public service costs, metered electricity and water use, and actual generator operation. These records do not establish a complete benefit-cost balance or causal community effect.
