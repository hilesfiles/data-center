# Draft public-records request — Digital Crossroad local-labor reporting

To: City of Hammond Department of Planning and Development / Hammond Redevelopment Commission records custodian

Subject: Existing records submitted under Section 1.9(B) of the November 1, 2018 DX Hammond OpCo development agreement

Please provide electronic copies of existing records created, retained, submitted, or received under Section 1.9(B) of the November 1, 2018 Development Agreement among the City of Hammond, Hammond Redevelopment Commission, Hammond Economic Development Commission, and DX Hammond OpCo, LLC for the Digital Crossroads of America Data Center and Technology Hub.

The request covers the initial Proof of Concept Phase and any later construction treated as part of that agreement. It seeks:

1. Quarterly compliance reports or data submissions provided under Section 1.9(B)(2), from construction commencement through the final submission or construction completion.
2. Records identifying bid notices or requests for proposals, bidders, successful contractors and subcontractors, their principal places of business, and documented reasons for rejecting otherwise compliant bidders from Hammond, Lake County, or elsewhere in Indiana, as described in Section 1.9(B)(1).
3. Records reporting construction-worker participation by residence or hiring-priority geography, including Hammond, Lake County, elsewhere in Indiana, and outside Indiana, if those categories were maintained.
4. Existing records of worker counts, hours, payroll, wages, trade classifications, apprenticeship status, or supervisory and nonsupervisory classifications included in or attached to the quarterly submissions.
5. Redevelopment Commission correspondence, staff memoranda, meeting materials, deficiency notices, certifications, or other reviews concerning compliance with Section 1.9(B).

This request seeks existing records and does not ask the City to create a new calculation, summary, or analysis. Aggregate or redacted records are acceptable where needed to avoid disclosure of personal identifiers. Please preserve the reporting period, contractor or subcontractor, worker geography, measure, unit, and any revisions when those fields exist.

If the records are held by another City department, commission, outside administrator, bond trustee, or contractor, please identify the custodian or forward the request as appropriate. If any portion is withheld, please identify the record or category withheld and the applicable basis for withholding.

Electronic delivery in the records' native format is preferred.

## Internal research note

This draft is designed to retrieve source records needed to measure realized construction labor and local participation. It should not be represented as a request for the City to estimate economic impact. Before sending, add the requester's name and contact information and confirm the City's current submission channel.
