# Application release 1.47 — contribution-account batch 1

Application release 1.47 advances the generated study data to `private-sector-study-1.44.0` and completes the first frozen orchestration batch: Meta Forest City / Rutherford County, Microsoft San Antonio / Bexar County, and EdgeConneX DET01 / Oakland County.

The study now publishes 595 sourced economic records—539 reported observations and 56 forecasts—and 154 separately governed modeled syntheses. Six of 36 projects pass the machine-enforced completed-contribution-account gate; 30 remain in the frozen queue. The batch adds six reported records, four forecasts, and 50 modeled syntheses. Modeled values remain excluded from canonical claims, sourced-record counts, and realized-benefit totals.

Each new account separates broad capital from construction-eligible spending, exposes transferred coefficients and assumptions, keeps direct, indirect, induced, and total channels non-additive outside declared aggregations, and labels sensitivity envelopes as non-statistical. County GDP, employment, and wage comparisons use `benchmark_application`, carry no causal-design metadata, and state that they are descriptive rather than data-center effects.

Meta Forest City and EdgeConneX DET01 publish same-period local-tax contribution and public-service-cost break-even thresholds without asserting actual service cost or a net fiscal result. Microsoft San Antonio does not publish a local net or break-even value because verified same-scope annual receipts and marginal public costs remain unavailable. All three accounts preserve unresolved evidence gaps rather than manufacturing observations.

Validation covers the full data contract, repository tests, TypeScript compilation, the production build, browser checks, release-count assertions, and whitespace integrity. The orchestration ledger records worker provenance, additions, validation, integration, and remaining analytical limits for each account.
