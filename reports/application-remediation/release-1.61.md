# Application release 1.61

Application release 1.61 advances the generated study data to `private-sector-study-1.57.0` and publishes the independently audited Google Douglas County, Equinix Seattle SE3, and Apple Prineville accounts.

The batch adds 139 source records—132 reported observations and seven source projections—and 21 separately governed last-resort modeled syntheses. The study now contains 1,230 source records—1,112 reported and 118 projected—and 146 modeled syntheses. Thirty completed research audits are map-eligible; analytical completeness remains a separate gate and no project currently passes it.

Google Douglas County includes 35 sources and 74 scoped records. An independent browser audit recovered eleven years of ordinary tax bills for both personal-property accounts, payment totals and dates for taxable account P88150, and fair-market-value history for exempt authority account P79020. Bills and their corresponding payments remain non-additive views of the same obligation; PILOT and lease payments, recipient distributions, and same-scope public costs remain unresolved.

Equinix Seattle SE3 includes 27 sources, 21 direct records, and eight bounded models. The account preserves building, campus, award, labor, tax, utility, security, energy, and emissions boundaries and does not relabel conditional or benchmark values as observed facility outcomes.

Apple Prineville includes 28 sources, 44 direct records, and five bounded models. The account distinguishes the selected building from the Prineville campus and later phases while preserving directly reported fiscal, construction, workforce, resource, and community observations separately from modeled sensitivities.

Each project contains exactly one factual project description. The project pages render “About this project” above “Why this project is in the study,” and publication verification covers every completed map marker plus direct project and county navigation.
