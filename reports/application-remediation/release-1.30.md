# Study release 1.30 - Hammond qualified investment and certified state incentives

Release `private-sector-study-1.30.0` publishes 496 source-checked economic records across all 36 candidates: 455 reported records and 41 source forecasts. The thirtieth evidence batch adds four Digital Crossroad Hammond observations from the current IEDC Transparency Portal and its underlying contracts.

## Hammond state investment and incentive account

- IEDC project 419963 reports $186,954,143 of actual qualified investment under the Data Center Tax Exemption Agreement.
- The same DATA record reports $28,369,398.27 paid or certified to date. This is a cumulative state sales/use-tax exemption, not a cash grant, local fiscal cost or project expenditure.
- The related Industrial Recovery Tax Credit agreement sets a $9,045,773.82 maximum credit; the current portal reports the full amount paid or certified to date. The contractual ceiling remains a forecast record while the cumulative certification is reported activity.
- The DATA contract describes the original plan as a three-phase, $200 million, 400,000-square-foot, 40 MW development with a $150 million minimum qualified investment. The application retains the existing $200 million forecast and does not convert the other design terms into realized operating scale.

The portal's $47,505,944.56 expected-investment field conflicts with the DATA agreement's $150 million minimum and the IRTC agreement's original investment figures. It remains in source notes and is not published as a project economic claim.

Hammond now has 72 records: 63 reported records and nine forecasts, plus 26 research updates.
