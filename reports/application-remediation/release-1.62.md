# Application release 1.62

Application release 1.62 advances the generated study data to `private-sector-study-1.58.0` and publishes the independently audited Meta Altoona, Microsoft Quincy, and Meta Fort Worth accounts.

The batch adds 257 source records—242 reported observations and 15 source projections—and 75 separately governed last-resort or source-model syntheses. The study now contains 1,487 source records—1,354 reported and 133 projected—and 221 modeled syntheses. Thirty-three completed research audits are map-eligible; analytical completeness remains a separate gate and no project currently passes it.

Meta Altoona adds 105 project-scoped source records and 17 models. The account retains parcel, permit, tax, incentive, workforce, electricity, water, community, and county-comparison evidence at its documented building, campus, county, program, or comparison scope. Five 2020–2024 campus water-withdrawal observations are canonical and approximately qualified because Meta reports the source table in whole megaliters.

Microsoft Quincy adds 46 project-scoped source records and 41 governed syntheses. Regional EY/IMPLAN results remain source-modeled, non-additive, and limited to Chelan, Douglas, and Grant counties; the twelve published industry values are preserved individually while their unresolved 884-versus-855 conflict remains explicit. Campus tax, infrastructure, water, compensation, and county-comparison records are not allocated to the selected building.

Meta Fort Worth adds 106 project-scoped source records and 17 models. The account preserves nine years of taxpayer-account evidence, cumulative tax arithmetic, fiscal break-even and abatement counterfactuals, payroll and resource sensitivities, and approximately qualified 2020–2024 campus water withdrawals. The county GDP comparison remains rejected because national and Texas-only donor universes reverse its sign.

Each project contains exactly one factual project description. The project pages render “About this project” above “Why this project is in the study,” and publication verification covers every completed map marker plus direct project and county navigation.
