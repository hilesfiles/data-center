# Application release 1.45 — economic-impact accounts

Application release 1.45 replaces the completed project and county pages' evidence-card dump with a structured economic-impact account. It does not change study release `private-sector-study-1.42.0`, the source records, modeled syntheses, or the three completed studies shown on the map.

Each completed account now leads with directly reported anchors, followed by construction-period contribution, annual operating contribution, the annual fiscal and infrastructure account, and estimated county effects. Every modeled value displays its central estimate, interval, period, confidence, and the label “Modeled synthesis · not observed or audited.” Construction totals, annual flows, fiscal balances, resource use, and county effects remain separate so users cannot mistake unlike measures for an additive benefit total.

The old “Annual-account coverage and remaining evidence” and “Analysis readiness” sections were removed. Source-level property, tax, forecast, and modeling records remain available in a collapsed audit ledger, while development chronology and the full bibliography are also subordinate to the account.

Completed county pages now embed the associated facility account and present the county context as four observed 2001–2024 series: real GDP, covered employment, population, and average weekly wage. A full 24-year table remains expandable for exact annual values. The summary baseline now emphasizes GDP, employment, population, personal income, wages, and per-capita income instead of internal record counts.

Mobile accounts use stacked metric records so the estimate, interval, period, and confidence remain visible without horizontal page scrolling. Browser validation covers all 39 existing and new presentation contracts, including the completed county account, the four county trends, the 24 annual rows, the absence of the discarded workflow sections, mobile overflow, and zero runtime errors.
