# Study release 1.16 - EdgeConneX DET01, Southfield

Release `private-sector-study-1.16.0` publishes 419 source-checked economic records across 33 of 36 candidates: 391 reported records and 28 source forecasts. This batch adds 48 records for EdgeConneX DET01 at 21005 Lahser Road in Southfield, Michigan.

## Added evidence

- Six final taxable-value stocks for the real parcel and the post-IFT commercial-personal account, tax years 2023-2025.
- Nineteen annual account charges and nineteen matching paid totals, retained separately for real parcel 76-24-33-476-055, IFT account 76-IP-13-000-088 and former-IFT commercial-personal account 76-99-43-004-025.
- State-approved forecasts of $16,251,600 in real-property investment and $18,935,500 in personal-property investment.
- City-reported forecasts of 27 full-time employees by 2015 and a $67,000 average annual salary.

The account histories are not consolidated. Bills and paid totals are account-wide amounts rather than recipient-level government receipts. The state investment amounts remain forecasts because a final-cost report was not located; later ownership, taxable-value and payment records do not verify the original employment or wage plan.
