# Study release 1.36 - Apple Mesa county-depth account

Release `private-sector-study-1.36.0` publishes 569 source-checked economic records across all 36 candidates: 518 reported observations and 51 source forecasts. The thirtieth evidence batch adds a nine-year operating-electricity series, renewable-resource capacity, individually scoped permit valuations, a cumulative investment statement, original job and investment promises, and a bounded state tax-credit estimate for Apple Mesa.

## Operating and construction account

- Apple reports Mesa electricity use rising from 45 million kWh in fiscal 2017 to 563 million kWh in fiscal 2025. The nine annual observations remain operator-reported facility use, not utility-meter verification, peak demand, cost or emissions.
- The 50 MW Bonnybrooke solar project and 4.67 MW of onsite PV are retained as separate nameplate-capacity stocks. They are not data-center load, annual generation or capital spending.
- Eight Mesa permit rows document data halls, two Tilden AZ4 expansion scopes and later air, cooling and water-system work. Each finaled or certificate-issued valuation remains an individual administrative record; related addenda and open Issued-only rows are excluded, and no permit values are summed.
- Mesa meeting minutes report about $2 billion already invested by December 1, 2020. That source-attributed cumulative amount is not an audited annual spending schedule and is not derived from the parcel assessments or permit values.

## Forecast and incentive account

The release preserves the original $2 billion ten-year investment promise, 150 full-time-job expectation and upper bound of 500 construction and trade jobs as forecasts. Arizona legislative staff separately estimate a $25 million, five-year income-tax-credit cost absent recapture. The taxpayer's fiscal 2019–2023 claim amounts are confidential, so no realized credit amount is published.

Mesa's 2026 FTZ operator agreement confirms that the same premises support data-center operations and data-center-equipment activities. It states activation and annual administration fees, but the reviewed form does not verify execution dates or payments and supplies no FTZ tax-benefit amount. The real-property parcel and Apple business-personal-property account remain separate throughout.

Apple Mesa now has 80 economic records: 76 reported observations and four source forecasts. It has five research updates.

## Remaining gaps and risks

The largest remaining gaps are current direct and contractor employment, wages, payroll, supplier payments and local shares, annual audited capital spending, realized state-credit and FTZ-benefit amounts, recipient-level net fiscal effects, measured water use, electricity cost and public-service costs. Apple's electricity series is operator reported; Mesa permit valuations are not expenditures; the approximate $2 billion actual and the original $2 billion plan are not a formal reconciliation; public incentives cannot be netted against tax bills without confidential claim and FTZ-benefit records.
