# Licensing and redistribution

No project-wide software or database license has been selected yet.

The PNNL/IM3 Atlas is described by the project specification as ODbL-derived. Its
attribution and share-alike implications must be reviewed before publishing an adapted
facility database. Software and database licensing may need to remain separate.

Copyrighted news bodies are never public data merely because they were accessible to an
acquisition pipeline or archive. Public artifacts may contain source metadata, URLs,
archive URLs, hashes, short legally appropriate excerpts, structured claims, and derived
features. Full text requiring local analysis belongs in a gitignored local cache.

Every public dataset manifest must state its license, redistribution status, and required
attribution before publication.

The county boundary artifact is U.S. government data published by the U.S. Census Bureau.
Its dataset manifest records redistribution as allowed and carries the attribution
`Source: U.S. Census Bureau` into the public map.
