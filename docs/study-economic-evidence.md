# Economic evidence acquisition and review

Reviewed 5 September 2026. Application study release `private-sector-study-1.39.0` publishes 584 source records (532 reported and 52 source projections) and separately publishes 13 modeled syntheses for Apple Mesa under the generic `study-modeled-synthesis-2.2.0` contract.

## Modeled synthesis boundary

Modeled syntheses live in `config/v1/study-modeled-synthesis.json`, outside the source-evidence register and canonical claim artifact. The companion `config/v1/study-modeling-policy.json` requires direct-evidence search first, separate preservation of source projections, decision relevance, cited inputs, reproducible formulas, defensible scope, disclosed uncertainty and a documented unresolved-gap fallback. Every synthesis is visibly labeled, includes an explicitly typed interval, named parameters with claim/source/assumption provenance, contribution channel, anti-overlap aggregation identity, formula, assumptions, limitations, unresolved evidence gap and qualitative confidence rationale. Sensitivity envelopes and deterministic counterfactuals are not statistical confidence intervals.

The synthesis layer covers Apple Mesa water use and cooling-treatment savings potential, zone-wide FTZ employment and payroll, explicitly acreage-allocated Apple-only employment and payroll, an electricity-cost benchmark, location-based gross electricity emissions, IOC tax-credit realization, a Class 1 property-tax counterfactual for tax years 2023–2025, and export-related duty avoidance. The Apple-only labor values use activated acreage only as an allocation key and remain low confidence. Water is benchmark-based rather than metered; the cooling scenario transfers a non-Mesa pilot result only as its high endpoint. Electricity cost uses statewide sector prices rather than Apple's tariff, and gross emissions do not net renewable procurement. Tax-credit and customs values remain sensitivities rather than disclosed benefits. No modeled value is counted among the 584 source records or treated as a causal effect.

## Persistence and publication

`config/v1/study-economic-evidence.json` is the reviewed, versioned input. It carries metric definitions, source metadata, retrieval date, available artifact SHA-256 hashes, fiscal/calendar/tax/source-year/snapshot/peak/cumulative/horizon timing, qualified amounts, scope, page or structured-field locators and interpretation notes. Downloaded source files are temporary review materials; the repository persists JSON facts and metadata, not copied publications.

`scripts/study_economic_evidence.py` checks the evidence contract and materializes the existing `source.schema.json` and `claim.schema.json` structures. Source claims use wider subject candidate keys where entity-level assignment remains unresolved. `accepted` on these claims means the document transcription was checked, not that an independent audit or project-boundary adjudication occurred. Claims retain provisional record status. The evidence-specific contract carries the accounting context that a bare numeric claim cannot represent.

`scripts/build_private_sector_study.py` publishes scoped records and sources within each project profile, derives coverage counts/categories, and hashes all inputs and outputs in the study manifest. Historical evidence and canonical inventory IDs are unchanged. No harmonized observation or metric-registry entry is emitted until the measure can be assigned a defensible subject and period; the batch’s source-measure definitions remain with its versioned evidence input.

## Rules for subsequent batches

1. Add a primary source and precise page/section locator. Preserve its reporting date, revision, audit status and extraction limitations. Record a file hash when a complete source was retrieved.
2. Identify what the quantity measures, who/where it covers, and whether it is a stock, flow or rate. Record taxpayer, campus or multi-campus scope without allocating it to one building by assumption.
3. Keep announced, projected and realized figures separate. A budget publication may contain all three. Use column headings and notes to determine the basis.
4. Preserve fiscal years, unknown snapshot dates and source-defined forecast horizons. Do not use the report’s title year as a workforce measurement year. Do not infer job-years, payroll or local residency from a headcount.
5. Add an annual series key only to exact, comparable reported activity for one metric, one subject scope and one year basis (fiscal, calendar or tax year). Leave missing years blank. Reconcile revised values before adding a second observation for the same period. Tax bills are distinct from cash receipts.
6. Do not duplicate one campus/company fact across project links, sum stocks across years, or combine investment, assessed value, payroll and taxes into one benefit dollar total. Do not subtract an abatement again from receipts already net of that abatement.
7. Record public costs and the payer alongside receipts. Restricted developer payments and grant-funded infrastructure spending need funding/reimbursement context. A gross expenditure is not automatically a public subsidy.
8. Advance analysis readiness only after a separate review of complete definitions, boundaries, dates and accounting coverage. Source collection alone does not establish attribution.
